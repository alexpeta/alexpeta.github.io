This page exists because Google asks for one before it will let a hobby app out of "testing" mode. Here is the whole policy.

**Peta Household** is a private, single-household application. It connects to the two Nest cameras at our house through Google's Smart Device Management API and records their video to a storage server inside the house.

- The only user is the owner of the house. Nobody else can sign in.
- Footage stays on the home server and is deleted after seven days.
- Nothing is uploaded, shared, sold, or sent to any third party.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of anyone.
- Revoking access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) stops the recording immediately.
